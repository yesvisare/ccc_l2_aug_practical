Understanding Text Embeddings

Text embeddings are dense vector representations of text that capture semantic meaning. Modern embedding models transform words, sentences, or documents into fixed-length vectors where similar texts have similar vector representations.

Evolution of Embeddings:
- Word2Vec (2013): First successful dense word embeddings
- GloVe (2014): Global vector representations
- FastText (2016): Subword-aware embeddings
- BERT (2018): Contextualized embeddings
- Sentence Transformers (2019): Sentence-level embeddings
- OpenAI ada-002 (2022): 1536-dimensional embeddings
- text-embedding-3-small (2024): 384-dimensional embeddings

Quality metrics include cosine similarity, clustering performance, and downstream task accuracy. Dimensionality affects both accuracy and computational efficiency.
